Forecasting U.S. Consumer Prices

Monthly model-driven forecasts of the headline Consumer Price Index, built from public economic data.

Latest Forecast

+0.156%
Predicted MoM change in U.S. consumer prices (headline CPI, seasonally adjusted) for Dec 2026

U.S. consumer prices (headline CPI, seasonally adjusted) are forecast to rise approximately +0.16% month-over-month in December 2026 — a modest, below-trend print that translates to roughly $780,000 in additional cost pressure on a $500 million annual cost base. That impact is not concentrated in one area: it ripples across pricing power, labor cost accruals, floating-rate debt service, and input procurement simultaneously. Executives should treat this as a low-intensity but broad-based inflation environment — one that rewards disciplined execution across all four levers rather than a single defensive move. The signal points to continued disinflation, not deflation, so complacency on structural cost management remains the primary risk.

Executive Recommendations

Forward-looking guidance for procurement and finance executives, grounded in the current forecast and market conditions. Refreshed each weekly model run.

Pricing Strategy

CFO / Commercial
With inflation running well below 0.2% MoM, aggressive price increases risk customer pushback and share loss — hold or micro-adjust list prices by no more than 0.1–0.2% where contracts allow. Use this low-inflation window to lock in multi-year customer agreements before any re-acceleration.

Compensation Planning

CHRO / CFO
A sub-0.2% monthly CPI print supports merit budgets in the 3.0–3.5% annualized range without real-wage erosion for employees. Avoid front-loading 2027 compensation commitments — preserve flexibility if the disinflation trend reverses in Q1.

Rate Exposure

CFO / Treasury
Subdued inflation reduces near-term Fed tightening risk, but the cumulative price trajectory over the past year remains elevated — don't unwind rate hedges prematurely. Maintain existing fixed-rate coverage on long-duration debt and review floating-rate exposure above 30% of total debt.

Purchasing & Inventory

COO / Supply Chain
Pacific Rim fabricated metal import prices remain an upward pressure point — consider locking in Q1 2027 contracts now while the broader inflation environment is soft. Lean inventory strategies are appropriate for most categories, but maintain a modest buffer on metal-intensive inputs given import price signals.

Current Driving Factors

Broad economic categories currently feeding the forecast. Each refreshes when the model retrains.

Import Price Pressures

pushing forecast up
Fabricated metal import prices from Pacific Rim suppliers are trending higher, adding modest upward pressure to goods inflation in the U.S. consumer price basket. This channel is particularly relevant for durable goods categories and downstream manufactured products.

Domestic Economic Growth

pushing forecast up
Real GDP growth continues to provide a mild demand-side floor under consumer prices, preventing a sharper deceleration in inflation. Sustained economic activity keeps services demand — and therefore services inflation — from falling more quickly.

Global Financial Conditions

pushing forecast down
Elevated corporate leverage in key trading-partner economies is signaling tighter global financial conditions, which historically dampen commodity prices and import costs flowing into U.S. consumer prices. This acts as a disinflationary counterweight to domestic demand strength.

Broad Consumer Price Momentum

mixed signal
Underlying consumer price components are sending mixed signals — some subcategories continue to decelerate while others remain sticky, particularly in shelter and services. The net effect is a muted but positive monthly print, consistent with a slow-moving disinflation path rather than a clean break lower.

Methodology

Target Variable

DEPVAR
Raw monthly delta of CPIAUCSL (headline CPI, seasonally adjusted) as published by BLS — not interpolated. BLS performs the seasonal adjustment, so the model trains on the published series directly.

Feature Universe

~21,000 series-derivative features
Constructed from FRED economic data: trailing values, differences, and percent changes at intervals of 30, 60, 120, 240, and 480 days.

Modeling

SwarmGA — multi-agent genetic search
Pre-selects 200 candidate features via random forest importance + univariate scoring; a swarm of agents searches feature subsets to maximize a held-out fitness metric.

Backtesting Protocol

Walk-forward, no future leakage
Each model is trained only on data available before the target date. Forward features are constructed from the cutoff and shifted by the forecast horizon.
