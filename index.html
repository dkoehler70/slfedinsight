Current Driving Factors

Broad economic categories currently feeding the forecast. Each refreshes when the model retrains.

Consumer prices rising

pushing forecast up
Broad consumer price pressures—spanning housing costs, personal care, and everyday goods—signal persistent inflation that lifts insured values and claims costs.

Import and input costs

mixed signal
Rising import prices for paper and agricultural goods push replacement costs higher, while falling auto-parts import prices offer modest offset for vehicle-related claims.

Labor costs in utilities

pushing forecast up
Elevated utility-sector wages increase operating expenses for insured businesses and raise the cost of post-loss restoration involving energy infrastructure.

Air travel and commerce

pushing forecast up
Expanding air transportation activity signals greater commercial exposure, driving higher demand for aviation and business interruption coverage.

Household balance sheets

pushing forecast down
A declining share of nonfinancial assets held by upper-wealth households suggests softening high-net-worth exposure, which could temper premium growth at the top end.

Methodology

Target Variable

DEPVAR
Raw monthly delta of WPU411 (Insurance PPI) as published by BLS — not interpolated, with a trailing-5-year seasonal mean removed before training and added back at prediction.

Feature Universe

~21,000 series-derivative features
Constructed from FRED economic data: trailing values, differences, and percent changes at intervals of 30, 60, 120, 240, and 480 days.

Modeling

SwarmGA — multi-agent genetic search
Pre-selects 200 candidate features via random forest importance + univariate scoring; a swarm of agents searches feature subsets to maximize a held-out fitness metric.

Backtesting Protocol

Walk-forward, no future leakage
Each model is trained only on data available before the target date. Forward features are constructed from the cutoff and shifted by the forecast horizon.
